# The Future Indicative Passive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, δείκνυμι)*

---

## 1. What This Form Is

"It will be given", "it will be set up" — an action still to come, with the subject undergoing it.

The future passive is built on the **aorist passive stem**, so it costs nothing extra to learn: take ἐδόθην, keep δοθ‑, add ‑ήσομαι.

| Verb | Aorist passive | Future passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| δίδωμι | ἐδόθην | **δοθήσομαι** |
| τίθημι | ἐτέθην | **τεθήσομαι** |
| ἵστημι | ἐστάθην | **σταθήσομαι** |
| δείκνυμι | ἐδείχθην | **δειχθήσομαι** |
| ἀφίημι | ἀφείθην | **ἀφεθήσομαι** |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Paradigm: **δοθήσομαι** "I shall be given"

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **δοθήσομαι** | I shall be given |
| 2 sg | **δοθήσῃ** | you will be given |
| 3 sg | **δοθήσεται** | it will be given |
| 1 pl | **δοθησόμεθα** | we shall be given |
| 2 pl | **δοθήσεσθε** | you will be given |
| 3 pl | **δοθήσονται** | they will be given |

The endings are the ordinary future middle ones; ‑θησ‑ in front of them is what makes the form passive.

### 2.2 Infinitive and Participle

- Infinitive **δοθήσεσθαι** "to be going to be given"
- Participle **δοθησόμενος, ‑η, ‑ον** "about to be given"

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Built on the aorist passive** | If you know the sixth principal part you know this tense. No new stem is involved. |
| **2. No augment** | ἐδόθην has one because it is a past indicative; δοθήσομαι does not. |
| **3. Distinguish from the future middle** | δώσομαι "I shall give (for myself)" vs. δοθήσομαι "I shall be given". The θη is the difference. |
| **4. σταθήσομαι vs. στήσομαι** | σταθήσομαι "I shall be set up" (passive); στήσομαι "I shall stand / I shall set up for myself" (middle). |
| **5. Common in prophecy and law** | The future passive is the natural voice for what will be decreed, granted or handed over. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἡ χώρα τοῖς συμμάχοις δοθήσεται.** — "The land will be given to the allies."
- **οἱ νόμοι ὑπὸ τῆς βουλῆς τεθήσονται.** — "The laws will be laid down by the council."
- **τὸ τρόπαιον ἐν τῷ πεδίῳ σταθήσεται.** — "The trophy will be set up on the plain."
- **ταῦτα πᾶσι δειχθήσεται.** — "This will be shown to everyone."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Decrees and treaties** are full of these forms: what will be handed over, released or established.
2. **Agent with ὑπό + genitive**, exactly as in the aorist passive.
3. **Reading tip.** θησ before a middle ending, with no augment: future passive, whatever the conjugation of the verb.
