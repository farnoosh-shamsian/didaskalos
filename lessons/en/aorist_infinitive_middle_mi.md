# The Aorist Middle Infinitive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵημι)*

---

## 1. What This Infinitive Is

"To give of one's own", "to set for oneself" — the action named as a single complete act, performed by the subject in its own interest.

μι-verbs build it from the **root aorist**: the ending goes straight onto the bare stem, with no ‑σα‑ and no augment.

| Verb | Aorist middle indicative | Infinitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| δίδωμι "give" | ἐδόμην | **δόσθαι** |
| τίθημι "place" | ἐθέμην | **θέσθαι** |
| ἵημι "send" | (ἀφ)είμην | **(ἀφ)έσθαι** |
| ἵστημι "set up" | ἐστησάμην | **στήσασθαι** (sigmatic) |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation

**bare aorist stem + ‑σθαι.** The stem vowel is short: δο‑, θε‑, ἑ‑.

### 2.2 The Compounds

In prose the compound is almost always the word you meet, and the preposition carries the meaning:

| Compound | Infinitive | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ἀποδίδωμι | **ἀποδόσθαι** | to sell |
| ἐπιτίθημι | **ἐπιθέσθαι** | to attack |
| συντίθημι | **συνθέσθαι** | to agree, to make a compact |
| προτίθημι | **προθέσθαι** | to set before oneself, to propose |
| ἀφίημι | **ἀφέσθαι** | to let go, to give up |

### 2.3 Accent

The accent sits on the first syllable of the simple form (δόσθαι, θέσθαι) and recedes no further than the preposition in compounds: **ἀποδόσθαι**, **ἐπιθέσθαι**.

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Aorist δόσθαι vs. present δίδοσθαι** | The present keeps the reduplicating δι‑. Remove it and you have the aorist. |
| **2. No augment, no sigma** | ἐδόμην is the indicative; δόσθαι has neither the ἐ‑ nor a ‑σα‑. |
| **3. ἵστημι is the odd one out** | Its middle aorist is sigmatic (ἐστησάμην, στήσασθαι) because it is transitive: "to set up for oneself". |
| **4. Middle, not passive** | The passive infinitive is δοθῆναι "to be given". ‑σθαι here means the subject acts for itself. |
| **5. ἀποδόσθαι is "to sell"** | Not merely "to give back" — that is ἀποδοῦναι, the *active* infinitive. The voice changes the meaning. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἐβούλετο τὴν οἰκίαν ἀποδόσθαι.** — "He wanted to sell the house."
- **ἔδοξεν αὐτοῖς συνθέσθαι πρὸς τοὺς πολεμίους.** — "They resolved to make terms with the enemy."
- **ἐπεχείρησαν νυκτὸς ἐπιθέσθαι τῷ στρατοπέδῳ.** — "They attempted to attack the camp by night."
- **οὐκ ἤθελε τῶν ὅπλων ἀφέσθαι.** — "He was unwilling to give up his arms."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **After verbs of wishing and deciding** — βούλομαι, ἐθέλω, ἔδοξεν αὐτοῖς — this infinitive completes the sense.
2. **συνθέσθαι** is the standard verb of treaty-making in the historians, alongside σπονδὰς ποιήσασθαι.
3. **Reading tip.** A short stem plus ‑σθαι with no reduplication and no augment: that is the μι-verb aorist middle infinitive, and the preposition in front will tell you what it means.
