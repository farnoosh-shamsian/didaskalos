# The Aorist Subjunctive Middle/Passive (ω-Verbs)
*(e.g., λύω, γίγνομαι, φοβέομαι)*

---

## 1. What This Form Is

The subjunctive is the mood of what *may* or *should* happen: purpose clauses, prohibitions, deliberations, general conditions. The aorist subjunctive views that possible action as **one complete act**, not as a process — a matter of aspect, never of past time.

As everywhere in the aorist, middle and passive are kept apart:

| Voice | Formation | 3 sg | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Middle | ‑σω‑/‑ση‑ + primary middle endings | **λύσηται** | that he free for himself |
| Passive | ‑θῶ, ‑θῇς, ‑θῇ… | **λυθῇ** | that he be freed |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 The Aorist Subjunctive Middle: **λύσωμαι**

**aorist stem + ‑σ‑ + long thematic vowel (ω/η) + primary middle endings.** No augment: the subjunctive never has one.

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **λύσωμαι** | (that) I free for myself |
| 2 sg | **λύσῃ** | (that) you free |
| 3 sg | **λύσηται** | (that) he/she free |
| 1 pl | **λυσώμεθα** | (that) we free |
| 2 pl | **λύσησθε** | (that) you (pl.) free |
| 3 pl | **λύσωνται** | (that) they free |

Thematic aorists drop the σ: γένωμαι, γένῃ, γένηται, γενώμεθα, γένησθε, γένωνται.

### 2.2 The Aorist Subjunctive Passive: **λυθῶ**

The passive stem in ‑θε‑ contracts with the long thematic vowel, giving circumflexed endings throughout:

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **λυθῶ** | (that) I be freed |
| 2 sg | **λυθῇς** | (that) you be freed |
| 3 sg | **λυθῇ** | (that) he/she/it be freed |
| 1 pl | **λυθῶμεν** | (that) we be freed |
| 2 pl | **λυθῆτε** | (that) you (pl.) be freed |
| 3 pl | **λυθῶσι(ν)** | (that) they be freed |

These are the *active* endings on a passive stem — the same pattern as the aorist passive indicative.

### 2.3 Where It Is Used

| Construction | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose (ἵνα, ὅπως, ὡς) | ἵνα λύσωνται τοὺς αἰχμαλώτους "so that they may ransom the prisoners" |
| Prohibition (μή + 2nd person) | μὴ φοβηθῇς "do not be afraid" |
| Deliberative question | τί γένωμαι; "what is to become of me?" |
| Hortatory (1st person plural) | γενώμεθα ἄνδρες ἀγαθοί "let us be brave men" |
| General conditions (ἐάν + subj.) | ἐὰν λυθῇ, ἀπελεύσεται "if he is freed, he will go away" |

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. No augment** | ἐλυσάμην and ἐλύθην are indicative. The subjunctive has λύσωμαι, λυθῶ — never *ἐλύσωμαι. |
| **2. Long vowel is the marker** | Indicative λύεται, subjunctive λύηται; indicative λύσεται (future), subjunctive λύσηται. The lengthened ω/η is what makes a form subjunctive. |
| **3. λύσῃ is ambiguous** | It can be aorist subjunctive middle 2 sg, aorist subjunctive active 3 sg, or future indicative middle 2 sg. Context and construction decide. |
| **4. Prohibitions prefer the aorist subjunctive** | μὴ ποιήσῃς "don't do it" (a single act) rather than an aorist imperative, which does not exist in the negative. |
| **5. Aspect, not time** | ἵνα λυθῇ means "so that he may be freed", present or future in reference. The aorist says the freeing is viewed as one event. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἐὰν λυθῶσιν, οἴκαδε ἐπανίασιν.** — "If they are freed, they will return home."
- **μὴ φοβηθῇς τοὺς πολεμίους.** — "Do not fear the enemy."
- **τί γένωμαι; ποῖ τράπωμαι;** — "What am I to become? Where am I to turn?"
- **ἥκομεν ἵνα βουλευσώμεθα περὶ τῆς εἰρήνης.** — "We have come in order to deliberate about the peace."
- **φοβοῦμαι μὴ ἡ πόλις ἁλῷ.** — "I am afraid the city may be taken."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **After verbs of fearing**, μή introduces what one fears may happen: φοβοῦμαι μὴ λυθῇ "I fear he may be released."
2. **In general conditions** with ἐάν and in indefinite relative clauses with ἄν, the subjunctive is compulsory, and the aorist is chosen whenever the action is thought of as complete.
3. **Reading tip.** A long ω/η in the ending with no augment in front means subjunctive; add ‑θ‑ before it and you have the passive.
