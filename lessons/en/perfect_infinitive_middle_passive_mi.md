# The Perfect Middle/Passive Infinitive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, δείκνυμι, δύναμαι)*

---

## 1. What This Infinitive Is

"To have been given", "to stand established" — the action named as complete, with its result still in force, and the subject either undergoing it or acting for itself.

| Verb | Perfect middle/passive | Infinitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| δίδωμι | δέδομαι | **δεδόσθαι** |
| τίθημι | τέθειμαι | **τεθεῖσθαι** |
| δείκνυμι | δέδειγμαι | **δεδεῖχθαι** |
| δύναμαι | δεδύνημαι | **δεδυνῆσθαι** |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation

**reduplicated stem + ‑σθαι**, attached directly with no thematic vowel, and with the accent on the penult — never recessive.

### 2.2 Accent Distinguishes Present from Perfect

This is the practical point of the lesson:

| Tense | Form | Accent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present | **δίδοσθαι** | recessive (antepenult) |
| **Perfect** | **δεδόσθαι** | on the penult |

Both end in ‑οσθαι; the reduplication vowel (δι‑ vs. δε‑) and the accent are what tell them apart.

### 2.3 Consonant Stems

δείκνυμι shows assimilation before the ending: δεικ‑ + σθαι → **δεδεῖχθαι**.

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Reduplication with ε** | δε‑δόσθαι, τε‑θεῖσθαι. The present reduplicates with ι (δίδοσθαι, τίθεσθαι). |
| **2. Accent on the penult** | δεδόσθαι, τεθεῖσθαι, δεδυνῆσθαι — fixed, unlike the recessive present. |
| **3. No augment** | An augment would make it the pluperfect indicative ἐδέδοτο. |
| **4. Indirect statement keeps the tense** | φησὶ δεδόσθαι "he says it has been given". |
| **5. δεδυνῆσθαι is active in meaning** | "To have been able" — δύναμαι is middle-only. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **λέγεται τὴν χώραν τοῖς συμμάχοις δεδόσθαι.** — "The land is said to have been given to the allies."
- **ὁμολογοῦσι τοὺς νόμους καλῶς τεθεῖσθαι.** — "They agree the laws have been well established."
- **φησὶ ταῦτα σαφῶς δεδεῖχθαι.** — "He says this has been clearly demonstrated."
- **οὐκ ἔφη δεδυνῆσθαι ἐλθεῖν.** — "He said he had not been able to come."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Indirect statement** after φημί, λέγεται and ὁμολογέω is where you will meet these forms.
2. **δεδεῖχθαι in philosophy**: "it has been shown" closes a proof.
3. **Reading tip.** Reduplication with ε, ‑σθαι, and an accent on the penult: perfect middle/passive infinitive.
