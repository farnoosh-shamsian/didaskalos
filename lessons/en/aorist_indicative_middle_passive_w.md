# The Aorist Indicative Middle/Passive (ω-Verbs)
*(e.g., λύω, βούλομαι, γίγνομαι)*

---

## 1. What This Form Is

In the present and imperfect a single set of endings covers both middle and passive. **In the aorist it does not**: the two voices are built on different stems and look nothing alike.

| Voice | Formation | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Middle | ‑σάμην, ‑σω, ‑σατο… | **ἐλυσάμην** | I freed for myself, I ransomed |
| Passive | ‑θην, ‑θης, ‑θη… | **ἐλύθην** | I was freed |

A row tagged "middle/passive" in the aorist is therefore telling you something specific: the token is either a **deponent** — a verb with middle forms and active meaning — or a form whose voice the annotation did not resolve. Both patterns are below; the sentence decides which you are reading.

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 The Aorist Indicative Middle: **ἐλυσάμην**

**augment + aorist stem + ‑σα‑ + middle endings**

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **ἐλυσάμην** | I freed for myself |
| 2 sg | **ἐλύσω** | you freed for yourself |
| 3 sg | **ἐλύσατο** | he/she freed for himself |
| 1 pl | **ἐλυσάμεθα** | we freed for ourselves |
| 2 pl | **ἐλύσασθε** | you (pl.) freed |
| 3 pl | **ἐλύσαντο** | they freed |

Note 2 sg **ἐλύσω**: the σ of the ending dropped between vowels and the vowels contracted (ἐλύσα‑σο → ἐλύσω).

Thematic aorists use the same endings without ‑σα‑: ἐγενόμην, ἐγένου, ἐγένετο; ἐλαβόμην, ἐλάβου, ἐλάβετο.

### 2.2 The Aorist Indicative Passive: **ἐλύθην**

**augment + stem + ‑θη‑ + secondary active endings**

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **ἐλύθην** | I was freed |
| 2 sg | **ἐλύθης** | you were freed |
| 3 sg | **ἐλύθη** | he/she/it was freed |
| 1 pl | **ἐλύθημεν** | we were freed |
| 2 pl | **ἐλύθητε** | you (pl.) were freed |
| 3 pl | **ἐλύθησαν** | they were freed |

The endings are *active* in shape — ‑ην, ‑ης, ‑η — even though the meaning is passive.

### 2.3 Deponents

Deponents use the middle set with active meaning, and this is where most "middle/passive" aorist tags come from:

| Verb | Aorist | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| γίγνομαι "become" | **ἐγενόμην** | I became |
| ἀποκρίνομαι "answer" | **ἀπεκρινάμην** | I answered |
| βούλομαι "want" | **ἐβουλήθην** | I wanted (passive form, active sense) |
| φοβέομαι "fear" | **ἐφοβήθην** | I was afraid |

βούλομαι and φοβέομαι show that some deponents take the ‑θη‑ forms without any passive meaning at all.

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. θ means passive form, not always passive sense** | ἐλύθη "was freed" but ἐβουλήθη "he wanted". Check whether the verb has an active voice at all before translating passively. |
| **2. Middle ≠ passive in the aorist** | ἐλυσάμην "I ransomed" vs. ἐλύθην "I was freed". Unlike the present, no form covers both. |
| **3. 2 sg ἐλύσω is a contraction** | Do not look for *ἐλύσασο. The same loss of intervocalic σ gives imperfect ἐλύου and future λύσῃ. |
| **4. The augment marks the indicative** | ἐλυσάμην (indicative) vs. λυσάμενος (participle), λύσασθαι (infinitive). Only the indicative has ἐ‑. |
| **5. The agent is ὑπό + genitive** | With a true passive: ἐλύθη ὑπὸ τοῦ στρατηγοῦ "he was freed by the general". |

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἐλύσαντο τοὺς αἰχμαλώτους πολλῶν χρημάτων.** — "They ransomed the prisoners for a great sum."
- **ἐλύθησαν οἱ δεσμῶται ὑπὸ τοῦ βασιλέως.** — "The prisoners were freed by the king."
- **ἐγένετο μάχη μεγάλη.** — "A great battle took place."
- **ἀπεκρίνατο ὅτι οὐκ εἰδείη.** — "He answered that he did not know."
- **ἐφοβήθησαν τοὺς πολεμίους.** — "They feared the enemy." (‑θη‑ form, active meaning)

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Narrative backbone.** ἐγένετο, ἀπεκρίνατο, ἐβουλήθη and their kin carry the story forward in Herodotus, Thucydides and the Gospels alike.
2. **Check the lexicon entry.** Whether a ‑θη‑ aorist is passive or deponent is a fact about the verb, not about the form; the dictionary form (‑ομαι with no active) settles it.
3. **Reading tip.** Augment + σα + ‑μην/‑το: middle. Augment + θη: passive form. Those two shapes cover almost every aorist token you will meet outside the thematic aorists.
