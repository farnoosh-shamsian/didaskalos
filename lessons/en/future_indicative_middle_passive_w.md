# The Future Indicative Middle/Passive (ω-Verbs)
*(e.g., λύω, βούλομαι, γίγνομαι)*

---

## 1. What This Form Is

"I shall free for myself", "I shall be freed". The future is the one tense that is purely about **time**: it has no aspectual partner and only one set of forms.

Middle and passive are distinct here, as they are in the aorist, but they share the same endings — the passive simply inserts ‑θησ‑ first:

| Voice | Formation | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Middle | stem + ‑σ‑ + middle endings | **λύσομαι** | I shall ransom |
| Passive | stem + ‑θησ‑ + middle endings | **λυθήσομαι** | I shall be freed |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Future Middle: **λύσομαι**

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **λύσομαι** | I shall ransom |
| 2 sg | **λύσῃ** (λύσει) | you will ransom |
| 3 sg | **λύσεται** | he/she will ransom |
| 1 pl | **λυσόμεθα** | we shall ransom |
| 2 pl | **λύσεσθε** | you (pl.) will ransom |
| 3 pl | **λύσονται** | they will ransom |

### 2.2 Future Passive: **λυθήσομαι**

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **λυθήσομαι** | I shall be freed |
| 2 sg | **λυθήσῃ** | you will be freed |
| 3 sg | **λυθήσεται** | he/she/it will be freed |
| 1 pl | **λυθησόμεθα** | we shall be freed |
| 2 pl | **λυθήσεσθε** | you will be freed |
| 3 pl | **λυθήσονται** | they will be freed |

The passive future is built on the aorist passive stem: ἐλύθην → λυθήσομαι, ἐγράφην → γραφήσομαι.

### 2.3 Deponent Futures

Many common verbs have no active future at all and use these middle forms with active meaning:

| Present | Future | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| βούλομαι | **βουλήσομαι** | I shall want |
| γίγνομαι | **γενήσομαι** | I shall become |
| ἀκούω | **ἀκούσομαι** | I shall hear |
| λαμβάνω | **λήψομαι** | I shall take |
| ὁράω | **ὄψομαι** | I shall see |

ἀκούω, λαμβάνω and ὁράω are active in the present but middle in the future — a quirk worth memorizing.

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. σ is the future marker** | λύ‑**σ**‑ομαι. In contract and liquid verbs the σ disappears and the endings contract: μενῶ, μενεῖται from μένω. |
| **2. Middle form, active meaning is common** | ἀκούσομαι means "I shall hear", not "I shall be heard" (that is ἀκουσθήσομαι). |
| **3. θησ marks the passive** | λύσομαι vs. λυθήσομαι. The extra θη is the whole difference. |
| **4. 2 sg λύσῃ is ambiguous** | It can be future middle 2 sg or aorist subjunctive middle 2 sg. Only context decides. |
| **5. No augment** | The future never has one; it refers forward. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **οἱ αἰχμάλωτοι λυθήσονται αὔριον.** — "The prisoners will be freed tomorrow."
- **οὐκ οἶδα τί γενήσεται.** — "I do not know what will happen."
- **ἀκούσεσθε τὴν ἀλήθειαν.** — "You will hear the truth."
- **βουλήσονται εἰρήνην ἄγειν.** — "They will want to keep the peace."
- **ταῦτα γραφήσεται ἐν τοῖς νόμοις.** — "This will be written in the laws."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Prophecy, promise and threat.** The future indicative is the mood of what is going to happen; in oratory it is the tense of both pledges and warnings.
2. **Deponent futures are a vocabulary matter.** Learn ὄψομαι, λήψομαι, εἴσομαι and γενήσομαι as principal parts, not as rules.
3. **Reading tip.** σομαι/σεται = future middle. θησομαι/θήσεται = future passive.
