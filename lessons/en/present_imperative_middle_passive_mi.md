# The Present Imperative Middle/Passive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, δύναμαι)*

---

## 1. What This Imperative Is

A command in the middle or passive: "give for yourself", "be given", "stand". The present stem commands the action **as a process** — keep doing it, or be in that state — where the aorist would command a single act.

| Form | Middle sense | Passive sense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **δίδοσο** | give of your own | be given |
| **τίθεσο** | set for yourself | be set |
| **ἵστασο** | stand, take your stand | be set up |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation

**present stem + middle imperative endings**, attached directly with no thematic vowel:

| Person | Ending | δίδωμι | τίθημι | ἵστημι |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 sg | ‑σο | **δίδοσο** | **τίθεσο** | **ἵστασο** |
| 3 sg | ‑σθω | **διδόσθω** | **τιθέσθω** | **ἱστάσθω** |
| 2 pl | ‑σθε | **δίδοσθε** | **τίθεσθε** | **ἵστασθε** |
| 3 pl | ‑σθων | **διδόσθων** | **τιθέσθων** | **ἱστάσθων** |

Later Greek uses ‑σθωσαν for the third plural.

### 2.2 The 2 sg Keeps Its σ

Thematic verbs lose the σ of ‑σο between vowels and contract (λύου from λύε‑σο). Athematic verbs do not: **δίδοσο**, **τίθεσο**, **ἵστασο**, **δύνασο**. This is the clearest single difference between the two conjugations in the imperative.

### 2.3 Compounds

| Compound | Form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ἀφίστημι | **ἀφίστασο** | revolt! stand away! |
| προτίθημι | **προτίθεσο** | set before yourself! |
| ἀποδίδωμι | **ἀποδίδοσο** | sell! |

The accent recedes but never past the preposition.

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. No augment** | ἐδίδοσο is the imperfect indicative ("you were being given"); δίδοσο is the imperative. |
| **2. σο, not ου** | The contraction that gives ω-verbs λύου does not happen here. |
| **3. Aspect, not politeness** | δίδοσο is a standing instruction; δοῦ (aorist) is "do it now, once". |
| **4. Prohibition with μή** | μὴ δίδοσο "don't keep giving"; for a single act, μὴ δῷ (aorist subjunctive). |
| **5. ἵστασο is intransitive** | "Stand", not "set something up" — the middle of ἵστημι turns the action back on the subject. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἵστασο παρὰ τὰς πύλας.** — "Take your stand by the gates."
- **μὴ ἀφίστασο τῶν φίλων.** — "Do not desert your friends."
- **τίθεσθε τὰ ὅπλα.** — "Ground your arms." (pl.)
- **διδόσθω ἑκάστῳ τὸ ἴσον.** — "Let an equal share be given to each."
- **προτίθεσθε τὸν λόγον.** — "Set out the argument before you."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Military and civic orders.** τίθεσθε τὰ ὅπλα is a set phrase for "ground arms"; διδόσθω is the language of allocation.
2. **Third-person imperatives** in ‑σθω are the standard form of impersonal instruction in decrees: "let it be done thus".
3. **Reading tip.** ‑σο, ‑σθω, ‑σθε, ‑σθων on a reduplicated athematic stem: present middle/passive imperative, and the reduplication (δι‑, τι‑, ἱ‑) is what marks it as present rather than aorist.
