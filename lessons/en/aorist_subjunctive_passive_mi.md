# The Aorist Subjunctive Passive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, ἀφίημι)*

---

## 1. What This Subjunctive Is

What may or should be done *to* the subject, once and completely: ἐὰν δοθῇ "if it is given", ἵνα τεθῇ ὁ νόμος "so that the law may be established".

The passive stem is the same ‑θε‑/‑θη‑ you know from ἐδόθην; the subjunctive lengthens the vowel and contracts, so every ending carries a circumflex.

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Paradigm: **δοθῶ** "that I be given"

| Person | Form | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **δοθῶ** | that I be given |
| 2 sg | **δοθῇς** | that you be given |
| 3 sg | **δοθῇ** | that it be given |
| 1 pl | **δοθῶμεν** | that we be given |
| 2 pl | **δοθῆτε** | that you be given |
| 3 pl | **δοθῶσι(ν)** | that they be given |

The same pattern on the other stems: **τεθῶ, τεθῇς, τεθῇ**…; **σταθῶ, σταθῇς, σταθῇ**…; **ἀφεθῶ, ἀφεθῇς, ἀφεθῇ**…

### 2.2 Where It Is Used

| Construction | Example |
| --- | --- |
| General condition (ἐάν) | ἐὰν ἡ χώρα δοθῇ "if the land is given" |
| Purpose (ἵνα, ὅπως) | ἵνα ὁ νόμος τεθῇ "so that the law be established" |
| Indefinite relative (ὃς ἄν) | ὃ ἂν δοθῇ "whatever is given" |
| Fear clause (μή) | φοβοῦμαι μὴ ἀφεθῇ "I fear he may be released" |
| Prohibition | μὴ ἀφεθῇς "do not be let go" |

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. No augment** | ἐδόθην is indicative; δοθῶ is subjunctive. |
| **2. Circumflex everywhere** | The contraction of ‑θε‑ with the long thematic vowel gives δοθῶ, δοθῇ, δοθῶσι. |
| **3. Active endings, passive sense** | ‑ῶ, ‑ῇς, ‑ῇ are the active subjunctive endings; the θ is what makes the form passive. |
| **4. δοθῇ vs. δῷ** | δοθῇ "that it be given" (passive); δῷ "that he give" (active) or "that you give for yourself" (middle). |
| **5. τεθῇ, not *θεθῇ** | Aspirate dissimilation, as throughout this verb's passive system. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἐὰν ἡ χώρα τοῖς συμμάχοις δοθῇ, πόλεμος ἔσται.** — "If the land is given to the allies, there will be war."
- **ἵνα οἱ νόμοι καλῶς τεθῶσιν.** — "So that the laws may be well established."
- **φοβοῦνται μὴ τὸ τρόπαιον μὴ σταθῇ.** — "They fear the trophy may not be set up."
- **ὃ ἂν δοθῇ, δέχου.** — "Accept whatever is given."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Conditions and purpose clauses** in decrees and treaties: what is to happen if something is granted or handed over.
2. **Fear clauses** with μή are frequent in the historians, where the thing feared is usually something being surrendered.
3. **Reading tip.** θ + circumflexed ω/η + no augment: aorist passive subjunctive, whatever the verb's conjugation.
