# The Future Middle/Passive Participle (ω-Verbs)
*(e.g., λύω, γίγνομαι, βούλομαι)*

---

## 1. What This Participle Is

"About to ransom", "about to be freed" — an action still to come, expressed as an adjective. Its chief job in Greek is to state **purpose**.

| Voice | Formation | Example | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Middle | ‑σόμενος | **λυσόμενος** | about to ransom |
| Passive | ‑θησόμενος | **λυθησόμενος** | about to be freed |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Both Sets

Each declines like a first/second declension adjective (ἀγαθός, ‑ή, ‑όν), with the accent fixed on the ‑ό‑ of ‑όμενος:

| Case | Middle | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nom. sg. m. | λυσόμενος | λυθησόμενος |
| Gen. sg. m. | λυσομένου | λυθησομένου |
| Nom. sg. f. | λυσομένη | λυθησομένη |
| Nom. sg. n. | λυσόμενον | λυθησόμενον |
| Nom. pl. m. | λυσόμενοι | λυθησόμενοι |

### 2.2 Deponents

Most future participles you meet belong to verbs whose future is middle in form but active in meaning:

| Verb | Future participle | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| γίγνομαι | **γενησόμενος** | about to happen |
| ἀκούω | **ἀκουσόμενος** | about to hear |
| μάχομαι | **μαχούμενος** | about to fight |
| ὁράω | **ὀψόμενος** | about to see |

### 2.3 Purpose with ὡς

- **ἦλθον ὡς μαχούμενοι.** — "They came in order to fight."
- **ἔπεμψαν πρέσβεις τὰ γενησόμενα πευσομένους.** — "They sent envoys to learn what was going to happen."

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. σ marks the future** | λυ‑**σ**‑όμενος (middle), λυ‑**θησ**‑όμενος (passive). Without σ you have the present λυόμενος. |
| **2. Purpose is the main use** | Especially after verbs of coming, sending and choosing, and unmistakably so with ὡς. |
| **3. τὰ γενησόμενα** | "The things that are going to happen" — a common way of saying "the future". |
| **4. No augment** | The future never takes one. |
| **5. Liquid futures contract** | μαχέομαι → μαχούμενος, μενέω → μενῶν: the σ drops and the vowels contract. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἦλθον ὡς λυσόμενοι τοὺς αἰχμαλώτους.** — "They came to ransom the prisoners."
- **οἱ λυθησόμενοι ἐν τῷ δεσμωτηρίῳ ἔμενον.** — "Those who were to be released were waiting in the prison."
- **οὐδεὶς οἶδε τὰ γενησόμενα.** — "No one knows what is going to happen."
- **ἀπέπλευσαν ὡς οὐ μαχούμενοι.** — "They sailed away, meaning not to fight."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **With the article** it names people by what is going to happen to them: οἱ λυθησόμενοι "those about to be freed".
2. **After verbs of motion** the future participle is the natural way to state an errand.
3. **Reading tip.** σόμενος = future middle; θησόμενος = future passive; όμενος alone = present.
