# The Future Passive Infinitive (ω-Verbs)
*(e.g., λύω, γράφω, πέμπω)*

---

## 1. What This Infinitive Is

"To be going to be freed." Like all future infinitives it carries **real time**, and it appears almost exclusively in indirect statement and after verbs of hoping, promising and expecting.

| Infinitive | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Present λύεσθαι | to be being freed |
| Aorist λυθῆναι | to be freed (one act) |
| **Future λυθήσεσθαι** | **to be going to be freed** |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation

**aorist passive stem + ‑θήσεσθαι** — that is, the future passive stem with the middle infinitive ending:

| Verb | Aorist passive | Future passive infinitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| λύω | ἐλύθην | **λυθήσεσθαι** |
| γράφω | ἐγράφην | **γραφήσεσθαι** |
| πέμπω | ἐπέμφθην | **πεμφθήσεσθαι** |
| ποιέω | ἐποιήθην | **ποιηθήσεσθαι** |

### 2.2 Where It Appears

| Construction | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Indirect statement | ἔφη λυθήσεσθαι "he said he would be freed" |
| Hoping, expecting | ἤλπιζον λυθήσεσθαι "they hoped to be released" |
| Swearing, promising | ὤμοσε μὴ προδοθήσεσθαι "he swore he would not be betrayed" |

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. θησ marks it** | λυ‑**θησ**‑εσθαι. Without the θη it is the future middle λύσεσθαι "to be going to ransom". |
| **2. Time, not aspect** | Unlike the present and aorist infinitives, this one really does refer to the future. |
| **3. No augment** | ἐλύθην has one; the infinitive does not. |
| **4. Compare the aorist** | λυθῆναι "to be freed" vs. λυθήσεσθαι "to be going to be freed". The σ and the middle ending mark the future. |
| **5. ἐλπίζω takes it naturally** | Verbs of hoping look forward, so the future infinitive is their default complement. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἤλπιζον οἱ αἰχμάλωτοι λυθήσεσθαι.** — "The prisoners hoped to be released."
- **ἔφασαν τὰ χρήματα ἀποδοθήσεσθαι.** — "They said the money would be given back."
- **οὐκ ᾤετο τὴν πόλιν ἁλώσεσθαι.** — "He did not think the city would be captured."
- **ἐνόμιζον ταῦτα ἐν στήλῃ γραφήσεσθαι.** — "They believed this would be inscribed on a stele."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Diplomacy and hope.** Whatever a speaker expects will be done to him or his city takes this form.
2. **After verbs of thinking** (οἴομαι, νομίζω) the future infinitive reports a prediction rather than a fact.
3. **Reading tip.** θησεσθαι at the end of a word: future passive infinitive, every time.
