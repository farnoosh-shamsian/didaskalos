# The Future Indicative Active (μι-Verbs)

---

## 1. What the Future Indicative Active Is

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Future** | An action that will take place: "I will give." |
| **Indicative** | The "statement" mood — for facts and ordinary questions. |
| **Active** | The subject performs the action. |
| **(mi)** | The athematic verbs: δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, ἵημι, δείκνυμι, φημί, and εἶμι. |

**The good news:** in the future, the μι‑verbs **abandon their athematic inflection**. The future is built with σ on the simple verb stem and conjugated **exactly like λύσω** — thematically.

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation: Verb Stem + σ + Thematic Endings

| Verb | Stem | Future | Meaning |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| δίδωμι | δο‑/δω‑ | **δώσω** | I will give |
| τίθημι | θε‑/θη‑ | **θήσω** | I will place |
| ἵστημι | στα‑/στη‑ | **στήσω** | I will set up |
| ἵημι | ἑ‑/ἡ‑ | **ἥσω** | I will send, throw |
| δείκνυμι | δεικ‑ | **δείξω** | I will show |
| φημί | φη‑ | **φήσω** | I will say |

*The future uses the **long grade** of the stem (δω‑, θη‑, στη‑) throughout — singular and plural alike.*

### 2.2 Full Paradigm: **δώσω**

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|------|--------|
| 1st | **δώσω** | **δώσομεν** |
| 2nd | **δώσεις** | **δώσετε** |
| 3rd | **δώσει** | **δώσουσι(ν)** |

θήσω, στήσω, ἥσω, δείξω conjugate identically.

### 2.3 Two Special Cases

| Verb | Future | Note |
|------|--------|------|
| **εἰμί** "to be" | **ἔσομαι** | Middle in form ("semi‑deponent"): ἔσομαι, ἔσῃ, ἔσται… |
| **εἶμι** "to go" | — | The present of εἶμι itself serves as the future "I will go" in Attic prose. |

---

## 3. Key Rules & Common Exceptions

| Rule | Explanation | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **1. Thematic conjugation** | The athematic endings disappear in the future. | δώσομεν like λύσομεν |
| **2. Long stem grade throughout** | No singular/plural alternation as in the present. | δώσω, δώσομεν (contrast δίδωμι/δίδομεν) |
| **3. σ + consonant contracts** | Stem‑final consonants combine with σ. | δείκνυμι → δείξω (κ + σ = ξ) |
| **4. ἔσται has no thematic vowel** | The 3rd sg. of ἔσομαι is irregular. | ἔσται "he will be" |
| **5. Compounds follow the simplex** | Prefix + same future. | ἀποδίδωμι → ἀποδώσω "I will give back" |
| **6. δύναμαι has a middle future** | The athematic deponents keep middle futures. | δυνήσομαι "I will be able" |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

| Greek | Translation |
|----------------------------|-------------|
| **δώσω σοι τὰ χρήματα.** | I will give you the money. |
| **οἱ στρατιῶται τρόπαιον στήσουσιν.** | The soldiers will set up a trophy. |
| **θήσομεν τοὺς νόμους ἐν τῇ ἀγορᾷ.** | We will place the laws in the marketplace. |
| **δείξει τὴν ὁδὸν τοῖς ξένοις.** | He will show the strangers the way. |
| **ἔσται ταῦτα.** | These things will be so. |

---

## 5. Usage & Context

1. **ἀποδώσω "I will pay back"** and its kin are everywhere in oratory and comedy — promises, contracts, threats.
2. **στήσω vs. στήσομαι:** the future active is transitive ("I will set X up"); the middle στήσομαι is intransitive ("I will stand"). The μι‑verbs make voice differences vivid.
3. **Spotting a μι‑verb future:** look for a bare long stem + σ (δωσ‑, θησ‑, στησ‑) without the present's reduplication (διδ‑, τιθ‑).
