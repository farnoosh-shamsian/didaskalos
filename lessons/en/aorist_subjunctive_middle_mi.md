# The Aorist Subjunctive Middle (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵημι)*

---

## 1. What This Subjunctive Is

What may or should be done, once and completely, by the subject for itself: ἐὰν ἀποδῶμαι "if I sell", ἵνα συνθώμεθα "so that we may come to terms".

μι-verbs build it from the **root aorist**: the bare short stem takes the long thematic vowel directly, which makes these forms very compact.

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Paradigms

| Person | δίδωμι | τίθημι | ἵημι (ἀφ‑) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 sg | **δῶμαι** | **θῶμαι** | **ἀφῶμαι** |
| 2 sg | **δῷ** | **θῇ** | **ἀφῇ** |
| 3 sg | **δῶται** | **θῆται** | **ἀφῆται** |
| 1 pl | **δώμεθα** | **θώμεθα** | **ἀφώμεθα** |
| 2 pl | **δῶσθε** | **θῆσθε** | **ἀφῆσθε** |
| 3 pl | **δῶνται** | **θῶνται** | **ἀφῶνται** |

ἵστημι is different: its middle aorist is sigmatic, so the subjunctive is στήσωμαι, στήσῃ, στήσηται.

### 2.2 Compounds

| Compound | Form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ἀποδίδωμι | **ἀποδῶμαι** | that I sell |
| ἐπιτίθημι | **ἐπιθῶμαι** | that I attack |
| συντίθημι | **συνθῶμαι** | that I agree |
| προτίθημι | **προθῶμαι** | that I propose |

### 2.3 Where It Is Used

Purpose clauses (ἵνα, ὅπως), general conditions (ἐάν), indefinite relatives (ὃς ἄν), prohibitions (μή + 2nd person), and hortatory first person plural: **συνθώμεθα** "let us make terms".

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. No augment** | ἐδόμην and ἐθέμην are indicative; δῶμαι and θῶμαι are subjunctive. |
| **2. δῷ is doubly ambiguous** | It is the 2 sg middle subjunctive *and* the 3 sg active subjunctive of δίδωμι. Only the construction distinguishes them. |
| **3. Aorist vs. present** | δῶμαι (aorist, single act) vs. διδῶμαι (present, ongoing). The reduplicating δι‑ is the difference. |
| **4. Prohibitions use this mood** | μὴ ἀποδῷ "do not sell", never an aorist imperative in the negative. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἐὰν τὴν οἰκίαν ἀποδῶμαι, ἀπελεύσομαι.** — "If I sell the house, I shall leave."
- **συνθώμεθα πρὸς ἀλλήλους.** — "Let us come to terms with one another."
- **φοβοῦμαι μὴ ἐπιθῶνται νυκτός.** — "I fear they may attack by night."
- **ἵνα μὴ τὰ ὅπλα προδῶνται.** — "So that they may not betray the arms."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Deliberation and resolve.** The hortatory subjunctive — θώμεθα, συνθώμεθα — is how a Greek speaker proposes a course of action to his own side.
2. **Fear clauses** after φοβοῦμαι μή are among the commonest homes of ἐπιθῶνται and its kin.
3. **Reading tip.** A very short word with a circumflexed ω or η and a middle ending: this is a μι-verb aorist subjunctive middle, and the preposition in front supplies the meaning.
