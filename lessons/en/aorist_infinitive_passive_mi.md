# The Aorist Passive Infinitive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, ἀφίημι)*

---

## 1. What This Infinitive Is

"To be given", "to be placed" — the action named as a single complete event that the subject undergoes.

μι-verbs form it on the same ‑θη‑ stem as the aorist passive indicative, so once you know ἐδόθην you know δοθῆναι:

| Verb | Aorist passive | Infinitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| δίδωμι "give" | ἐδόθην | **δοθῆναι** |
| τίθημι "place" | ἐτέθην | **τεθῆναι** |
| ἵστημι "set up" | ἐστάθην | **σταθῆναι** |
| ἀφίημι "release" | ἀφείθην | **ἀφεθῆναι** |
| δείκνυμι "show" | ἐδείχθην | **δειχθῆναι** |

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation

**short stem + ‑θῆναι**, with no augment and a circumflex always on the η.

### 2.2 Accent

The circumflex on ‑θῆ‑ never moves, however long the compound: ἀποδοθῆναι, ἐπιτεθῆναι, καταστᾰθῆναι. This makes the form easy to spot and impossible to confuse with the aorist active infinitive δοῦναι, θεῖναι, στῆναι.

### 2.3 Where It Appears

| Construction | Example |
| --- | --- |
| After verbs of wishing, ordering | ἐκέλευσε τὰ χρήματα ἀποδοθῆναι "he ordered the money to be given back" |
| Indirect statement | λέγεται δοθῆναι "it is said to have been given" |
| Result clause | ὥστε τὸν νόμον τεθῆναι "with the result that the law was laid down" |
| Articular infinitive | διὰ τὸ ἀφεθῆναι "because of being released" |

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. No augment** | ἐδόθην is indicative; δοθῆναι is the infinitive. |
| **2. Three aorist infinitives, three shapes** | Active δοῦναι, middle δόσθαι, passive δοθῆναι. All three are aorist; only the ending tells you the voice. |
| **3. τεθῆναι, not *θεθῆναι** | The first of two aspirates dissimilates, as everywhere in this verb's passive. |
| **4. Aspect, not time** | Except in indirect statement, δοθῆναι says only that the giving is viewed as one complete act. |
| **5. σταθῆναι vs. στῆναι** | σταθῆναι "to be set up" (passive, someone else acts); στῆναι "to stand" (intransitive root aorist active). |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **ἐκέλευσε τὰ χρήματα τοῖς συμμάχοις ἀποδοθῆναι.** — "He ordered the money to be given back to the allies."
- **ἐβούλοντο τοὺς αἰχμαλώτους ἀφεθῆναι.** — "They wanted the prisoners to be released."
- **λέγεται τὸ τρόπαιον ἐν τῷ πεδίῳ σταθῆναι.** — "The trophy is said to have been set up on the plain."
- **ἄξιόν ἐστι τὸν νόμον τεθῆναι.** — "It is right that the law be established."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Decrees and orders.** κελεύω, ψηφίζομαι and δοκεῖ + this infinitive is the standard way of recording what an assembly resolved should be done.
2. **Indirect statement** is the one context where it carries past time: λέγεται δοθῆναι "it is said to have been given".
3. **Reading tip.** ‑θῆναι with a circumflex is an aorist passive infinitive every time; strip it off and the short stem in front identifies the verb.
