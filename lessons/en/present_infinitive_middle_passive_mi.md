# The Present Middle/Passive Infinitive (μι-Verbs)
*(e.g., δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, δύναμαι)*

---

## 1. What This Infinitive Is

The present infinitive names an action as **ongoing or repeated**, without person or time. In the middle/passive it means either that the subject acts on or for itself, or that it undergoes the action:

| Form | Middle sense | Passive sense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **δίδοσθαι** | to give for oneself | to be given |
| **τίθεσθαι** | to set for oneself, to establish | to be set |
| **ἵστασθαι** | to stand, to place oneself | to be set up |

One set of forms serves both voices in the present — only the aorist and future keep them apart.

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation

**present stem + ‑σθαι**, with no connecting vowel. This is what makes μι-verbs simpler here than ω-verbs, which insert the thematic vowel ε: λύ‑**ε**‑σθαι but δίδο‑σθαι.

| Verb | Present stem | Infinitive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| δίδωμι "give" | διδο‑ | **δίδοσθαι** |
| τίθημι "place" | τιθε‑ | **τίθεσθαι** |
| ἵημι "send" | ἱε‑ | **ἵεσθαι** |
| ἵστημι "make stand" | ἱστα‑ | **ἵστασθαι** |
| δείκνυμι "show" | δεικνυ‑ | **δείκνυσθαι** |
| δύναμαι "be able" | δυνα‑ | **δύνασθαι** |
| ἐπίσταμαι "know how" | ἐπιστα‑ | **ἐπίστασθαι** |
| κεῖμαι "lie" | κει‑ | **κεῖσθαι** |

### 2.2 Accent

The accent is **recessive** — it falls back as far as the rules allow, giving δίδοσθαι, τίθεσθαι, ἵστασθαι, δύνασθαι. This is a real diagnostic: the perfect middle/passive infinitive of the same verbs accents the penult (δεδόσθαι, τεθεῖσθαι), so accent alone distinguishes present from perfect.

### 2.3 The Stem Vowel Is Short

The infinitive is built on the **short-vowel** form of the stem: δίδωμι but δίδο‑σθαι, τίθημι but τίθε‑σθαι, ἵστημι but ἵστα‑σθαι. The long vowel of the first person singular belongs to the singular indicative only.

---

## 3. Key Rules and Pitfalls

| Rule | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| **1. No thematic vowel** | δίδοσθαι, not *διδόεσθαι. Compare ω-verb λύεσθαι, where the ε is required. |
| **2. Short stem vowel** | δίδο‑, τίθε‑, ἵστα‑ throughout. The ω and η of δίδωμι, τίθημι never appear here. |
| **3. Present vs. perfect by accent and reduplication** | δίδοσθαι (present, recessive accent) vs. δεδόσθαι (perfect, reduplicated, accent on the penult). |
| **4. Middle-only verbs use it constantly** | δύνασθαι, ἐπίστασθαι, κεῖσθαι have no active at all; their infinitive is this form, and although the ending is middle/passive the meaning is active. |
| **5. Watch the compounds** | ἀποδίδοσθαι "to sell", προτίθεσθαι "to set before oneself", ἀφίστασθαι "to revolt" — the accent stays recessive but cannot move back past the preposition: ἀφίστασθαι, not *ἄφιστασθαι. |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

- **οὐ δύναμαι ταῦτα ποιεῖν.** — "I am not able to do this." (δύνασθαι in a finite form)
- **βούλονται τὴν χώραν ἀποδίδοσθαι.** — "They want to sell the land."
- **δεῖ τοὺς νόμους καλῶς τίθεσθαι.** — "The laws must be well established."
- **οἱ σύμμαχοι ἤρξαντο ἀφίστασθαι.** — "The allies began to revolt."
- **αἰσχρόν ἐστι μὴ ἐπίστασθαι γράμματα.** — "It is shameful not to know letters."

---

## 5. Usage and Context

1. **Complementary infinitive.** After βούλομαι, δύναμαι, ἄρχομαι, δεῖ and similar verbs, this is the form that completes the sense: ἤρξαντο ἀφίστασθαι.
2. **Articular infinitive.** With τό it becomes a noun: τὸ δίδοσθαι "the being given", διὰ τὸ μὴ δύνασθαι "because of being unable".
3. **Reading tip.** ‑σθαι at the end and no thematic vowel in front of it: that combination points straight at a μι-verb, and the stem you see is the one the whole present system is built on.
