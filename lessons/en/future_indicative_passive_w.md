# The Future Indicative Passive (ω-Verbs)

---

## 1. What the Future Indicative Passive Is

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Future** | An action that will take place. |
| **Indicative** | The "statement" mood — for facts and ordinary questions. |
| **Passive** | The subject **will receive** the action: "I will be loosed." |
| **(w)** | The thematic verbs in ‑ω (λύω) and the deponents in ‑ομαι. |

Unlike the present and imperfect, **the future distinguishes passive from middle**: λύσομαι "I will loose for myself" (middle) vs. **λυθήσομαι** "I will be loosed" (passive).

---

## 2. Forms and Patterns

### 2.1 Formation: Aorist‑Passive Stem (θη) + σ + Middle Endings

The future passive is built on the same **θη‑stem** as the aorist passive, plus **σ** and the primary middle endings with thematic vowel:

**λύω → λυθήσομαι "I will be loosed":**

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|------|--------|
| 1st | **λυθήσομαι** | **λυθησόμεθα** |
| 2nd | **λυθήσῃ / λυθήσει** | **λυθήσεσθε** |
| 3rd | **λυθήσεται** | **λυθήσονται** |

### 2.2 The Usual Stem Changes (as in the Aorist Passive)

| Verb | Aorist passive | Future passive | Meaning |
|------|----------------|----------------|---------|
| παιδεύω | ἐπαιδεύθην | **παιδευθήσομαι** | I will be educated |
| πείθω | ἐπείσθην | **πεισθήσομαι** | I will be persuaded |
| πέμπω | ἐπέμφθην | **πεμφθήσομαι** | I will be sent |
| γράφω | ἐγράφην | **γραφήσομαι** | I will be written (2nd passive, no θ) |
| φαίνω | ἐφάνην | **φανήσομαι** | I will appear (2nd passive) |

### 2.3 Passive Deponents Keep Their θη‑Future

Passive deponents form this future with **active meaning**:

| Verb | Future | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| **πορεύομαι** | **πορευθήσομαι** | I will travel |
| **φοβέομαι** | **φοβηθήσομαι** | I will fear |
| **ἰάομαι** | **ἰαθήσεται** | he will be healed / will recover |

---

## 3. Key Rules & Common Exceptions

| Rule | Explanation | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **1. No augment** | The augment belongs to the aorist indicative only; the future keeps the bare θη‑stem. | λυθήσομαι, not *ἐλυθήσομαι |
| **2. Middle endings, passive meaning** | The θη(σ) element carries the passive sense. | λυθήσεται "he will be loosed" |
| **3. Future middle ≠ future passive** | The future is the tense where the two voices split. | λύσεται (middle) vs. λυθήσεται (passive) |
| **4. Second passives lack θ** | Verbs with 2nd aorist passives form the future the same way. | γραφήσομαι, φανήσομαι |
| **5. Agent with ὑπό + genitive** | As in all passives. | πεισθήσεται ὑπὸ τῶν ῥητόρων |
| **6. Passive deponents translate actively** | The θη‑future of a deponent is not a passive in meaning. | πορευθήσομαι "I will travel" |

---

## 4. Example Sentences

| Greek | Translation |
|----------------------------|-------------|
| **οἱ αἰχμάλωτοι λυθήσονται.** | The prisoners will be released. |
| **ὁ δῆμος πεισθήσεται ὑπὸ τῶν ῥητόρων.** | The people will be persuaded by the orators. |
| **ἡ ἐπιστολὴ γραφήσεται.** | The letter will be written. |
| **ὁ παῖς ἰαθήσεται.** | The child will be healed. (passive deponent, active sense "will recover") |
| **πορευθησόμεθα εἰς τὴν πόλιν.** | We will travel to the city. (passive deponent) |

---

## 5. Usage & Context

1. **Prophecies, promises, and threats** live in the future passive: "it will be done," "he will be punished."
2. **Spotting the form:** θησ (or the 2nd‑passive ησ without θ) before middle endings is unmistakable — λυ‑**θησ**‑εται.
3. **In koine Greek** the future passive of deponents (πορευθήσομαι) becomes even more common; always check the headword before translating as a passive.
